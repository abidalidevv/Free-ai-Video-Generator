# ROUTES.md — Complete Route Catalogue

> Every route declared by `src/routes/*.tsx`. Source of truth:
> `src/routeTree.gen.ts` (auto-generated by TanStack Router).

**Phase 8.5 (final frontend consolidation):** `/settings` and
`/api-manager` were deleted and their contents redistributed; 10 legacy
account/library routes are now 307 redirects into consolidated
destinations; `/account` and the studios accept validated `?tab=`
parameters; `/help` is a documentation hub; `/login` offers "Continue as
Local Studio".

All routes are flat and unauthenticated. None use a `loader`; all data is
local state or seed constants from `src/lib/render-pipeline.ts` and
`src/lib/constants.ts`.
